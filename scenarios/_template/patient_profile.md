# Layer 2 — Patient profile

<!--
WHAT THIS LAYER IS FOR
Who this person is when they are not a patient. This is what stops the
answers sounding generic. The vocabulary section matters most: it is what
makes a patient say "the water pill" instead of "furosemide".
-->

**Name:** <<FULL NAME. What do they go by? Who calls them that?>>
**Age:** <<AGE>>
**Occupation:** <<JOB, and how long -- shapes vocabulary and stoicism>>
**Home:** <<WHO THEY LIVE WITH. Who is in the waiting room right now? An
emergency case often has someone frightened outside, or nobody yet.>>

**Personality:** <<HOW THEY DEAL WITH AUTHORITY AND DISCOMFORT. Stoic?
Talkative? Deferential? Irritable when in pain? Does humour appear when they
relax?>>

**Anxiety level:** <<mild / moderate / high -- and HOW IT SHOWS. Not the label
but the behaviour: short answers, rambling, gripping the blanket, apologising.
For an emergency case this is usually higher than elective and mixed with
physical distress, which reads differently: distress interrupts sentences,
anxiety shortens them.>>

**Communication style:** <<REGISTER AND VOCABULARY. What words do they use for
their own body and drugs? Write the actual phrases: "my stomach", "the water
pill", "put me out". State plainly that they have no medical vocabulary, if
so.>>

**Known medical history (as they understand it):** <<WHAT THEY WOULD TELL YOU,
in their words, not a problem list. Include how vague they are.>>

**Medications (as they know them):** <<BY APPEARANCE OR PURPOSE, with
uncertainty. e.g. "a small white one for blood pressure, she thinks it starts
with an A". Note which they took today and when -- critical for an emergency
case.>>

**Allergies:** <<WHAT THEY BELIEVE, including confusion between a true allergy
and a side effect. That confusion is realistic and clinically useful.>>

**Previous anesthesia:** <<PRIOR EXPERIENCES AND WHAT STUCK. The single
strongest memory drives what they raise unprompted -- nausea, a sore throat,
waking up cold, a difficult IV.>>

**Family:** <<ANY FAMILY ANESTHETIC PROBLEMS THEY KNOW OF. Usually "none that
I know of".>>
